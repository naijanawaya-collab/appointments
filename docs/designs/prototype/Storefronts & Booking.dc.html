Flow rules
  • 1 barber → step 2 skipped, progress shows 3 segments
  • No single barber offers all chosen services → inline note on step 2 with “Go back”
  • Focus moves to the step heading on every change
  • Date strip: today + 60 days, closed days dashed + struck
  • Sticky bar reserves its height up front → CLS 0
  • Back keeps all earlier choices; a 409 keeps services + details
